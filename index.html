Chapter One — The Beginning 🤎

At first, Youssef was just my friend’s brother’s friend. I used to see him at her place sometimes, and I never thought of him as anything more.

Until one day, I was feeling overwhelmed and went to talk to my friend about a problem. Youssef was there and heard me. From that day, he started checking on me and making sure I was okay.

Chapter Two — Getting Closer 🤎

With time, we started talking more. I began telling him about my problems and my life, and he was always there to listen and support me.

He would pick me up from my lessons and take me home. They were simple things, but they meant so much to me.

Without realizing it, we became closer and closer every day.

Chapter Three — Where Our Story Began ❤️

One day, at a gas station, something happened that changed everything.

Youssef told me that he loved me, and from that moment, our real story began.

That gas station became a very special place for us, because it witnessed the moment our love story started. Even today, we still go there and remember how it all began.

And from that day on, the most beautiful chapter of my life began. ❤️
